## The problem
We aim to compare PM10 levels across selected monitoring stations in Istanbul. We will examine seasonal patterns and weekday–weekend differences. Understanding these patterns can help us identify where and when air pollution is higher.

## The data
| Dataset | Link | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| Istanbul air quality monitoring data (planned source: IBB) | https://havakalitesi.ibb.gov.tr/ | Station-level PM10 measurements with timestamps. We plan to use one complete year of data from selected stations to compare spatial, seasonal, and weekday–weekend patterns. Historical data access is pending confirmation. |
## Our questions
1. How do average daily PM₁₀ concentrations differ across the selected air quality monitoring stations in Istanbul?
2. How do PM₁₀ concentrations vary across seasons, and are these seasonal patterns similar across stations?
3. Do daily PM₁₀ concentrations differ between weekdays and weekends when seasonal differences are taken into account?
